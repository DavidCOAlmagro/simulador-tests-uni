T: Theory
P: A classifier makes an independent decision for each image, and each decision ends its episode. Under that assumption, which property applies to the environment?
a) Necessarily partially observable.
b) Episodic.
c) Adversarial multi-agent.
d) Sequential, with effects carried over between decisions.
R: b
E: Cada decisión es independiente y cierra su episodio: entorno episódico.

P: UCS has just generated a goal with cost 13. Valid nodes with g=4 and g=9 are still on the frontier. Which rule preserves the usual guarantee for non-negative costs in a finite graph?
a) Prioritise the most recently generated node even if its g is higher.
b) Close the goal before comparing g-values.
c) Stop immediately when that goal is inserted.
d) Test for a goal on removal from the frontier, prioritising by g and keeping cost improvements.
R: d
E: UCS comprueba la meta al SACARLA, no al insertarla. Los nodos con g=4 y g=9 podrían llevar a una meta más barata que 13.

P: After forward checking on A=1, the value 1 is removed from B. Later, B=2 removes the value 2 from C. If only B=2 is undone, what is restored?
a) The value 2 in C, provided this was the only reason it was removed.
b) The value 1 in B, even though A remains assigned.
c) All original values of all variables.
d) Nothing, because pruning is permanent.
R: a
E: Al deshacer B=2 solo se restaura lo que quitó B=2: el 2 de C. El 1 de B lo quitó A=1, que sigue asignada.

b) Only after selecting the root move.

d) At a non-terminal node that has reached the depth limit.

P: An agent at S can stop for a reward of 4, or advance to T for a reward of 1. From T, the only action ends the episode with a reward of 12 or −2, each with probability 0.5. With V₀=0 and γ=0.75, what is V₂(S) and which action achieves it? Use synchronous updates.
![From Action To Prob. Reward; S stop Terminal 1 4; S advance T 1 1; T act Terminal 0.5 12; T act Terminal 0.5 -2](img/modelo_d_13.png)
a) 5, advance.
b) 4.75, advance.
c) 6, stop.
d) 4.75, stop.
R: b
E: V₁(T) = 0.5·12 + 0.5·(−2) = 5. V₂(S) = max(parar 4, avanzar 1 + 0.75·5 = 4.75) = 4.75, avanzar.
